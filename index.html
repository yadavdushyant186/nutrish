The Nutrish Standard

How True Nuts & Seeds Should Taste

Most supermarket nuts spend 18 months sitting in warm warehouses, chemically bleached to look bright white. Here is how Nutrish returns to pure, uncompromised harvest craft:

Zero Chemical Bleaching

We never use sulfur dioxide or chlorine gas to artificially whiten cashews and pistachios. You taste the true, earthy golden harvest with its protective skin nutrients intact.

Naturally sun-cured

Cold-Stored Below 14°C

Delicate omega fats oxidize in warm ambient air, causing that unpleasant bitter aftertaste in commercial walnuts. Our temperature-controlled cold rooms guarantee a fresh, sweet snap.

Prevents oil oxidation

Slow Artisan Dry Roasting

When we roast, we never submerge nuts in cheap palm or canola oils. We slow-tumble them in cast iron roasters with raw Himalayan rock salt to celebrate their natural essential crunch.

Zero added cooking oils
